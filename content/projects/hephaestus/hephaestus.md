Hephaestus is a Go-based engineering orchestration engine and companion development-tool plugin. It explores test-first task execution, independent review, deterministic quality gates, and recorded evidence of engineering decisions.

The project includes a task state machine, policy evaluation, provenance records, and controls around test and review workflows. Its integration and verification capabilities are evolving; it is not presented as a production-ready autonomous delivery system.
