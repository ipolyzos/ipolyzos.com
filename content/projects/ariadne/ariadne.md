Ariadne is a containerized platform for orchestrating multi-step software engineering workflows. It coordinates specialized engineering roles through explicit handoffs, policy gates, and persistent execution state.

Its architecture separates the conversational interface from a control plane built around workflow orchestration and PostgreSQL-backed state. Container isolation, verification evidence, and independent review are central design concerns. The project is at release-candidate stage, with documented limitations.
